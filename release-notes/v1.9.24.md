# Cheat.Guard v1.9.24

## Invigilator screen

- The **LAN IP** and **Exam code** on the monitor are now separate teal
  labels: clicking either copies its value to the clipboard (with a brief
  "(Copied)" flash), so sharing the join details with students is one click.
  Multiple LAN addresses are copied together, comma-separated; students use
  the one on their own network, or leave the IP field empty for auto-detect.
